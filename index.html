About Me

I am a hybrid professional with a unique blend of strategic and technical expertise. My passion lies at the intersection of understanding complex systems—whether they're markets or networks.

As a Market Researcher, I dive deep into data, analyze trends, and model consumer behavior to uncover strategic opportunities for growth.

As a Penetration Tester, I meticulously probe digital infrastructures, identify critical vulnerabilities, and provide actionable remediation strategies to fortify security postures.

This dual perspective allows me to not only help businesses find their audience but also ensure they can protect their data, build trust, and grow sustainably.

Core Services

01. Strategic Market Intelligence

Go beyond simple surveys. I provide a deep, analytical dive into your market to arm you with the intelligence needed to win.

  • Competitor & Landscape Analysis

    Mapping your ecosystem to find untapped niches, anticipate competitor moves, and define your unique value proposition.

  • Audience & Persona Modeling

    Using data-driven methods to build detailed, actionable personas. Understand *who* your customer is, *what* they need, and *how* to reach them.

  • Go-to-Market Strategy

    Developing a data-backed plan to launch your product or service, identifying the right channels, messaging, and pricing to ensure traction.

02. Proactive Security Fortification

Don't wait for a breach. I simulate real-world attacks to find and fix vulnerabilities before they can be exploited.

  • Web App & API Pentesting

    A comprehensive review of your digital platforms (OWASP Top 10, and beyond) to identify flaws like XSS, SQLi, and insecure access control.

  • Network & Infrastructure Audits

    Scanning and testing your cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure) and on-prem environments to find misconfigurations and unpatched systems.

  • Social Engineering Simulation

    Testing your human firewall. I design targeted phishing and vishing campaigns to train your team and harden your first line of defense.
